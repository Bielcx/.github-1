## What is Gnars? ⌐◨-◨

Gnars is a **Nounish, open-source action-sports collective** — a fork of the [Nouns Builder](https://nouns.build) protocol running on [Base](https://base.org). There's no company behind it and no brand manager approving campaigns: the community itself decides who gets funded, what gets built, and where the treasury goes. **1 GNAR = 1 vote.**

Everything is **CC0** — free to remix, fork, and build on, no permission needed.

## How it works

| Auctions | Governance | Sponsorships | Bounties |
|---|---|---|---|
| A new Gnar is auctioned continuously onchain. Winning bids flow straight into the DAO treasury. | Any Gnar holder can submit a proposal — sponsor a rider, fund a build, back an event. The community votes onchain. | Anyone can co-sponsor a rider through a staking vault: deposit stays yours, only the yield is shared (rider, treasury, staker). | Community-funded challenges (via [poidh](https://poidh.xyz)) pay athletes in ETH to land the tricks and stunts people want to see filmed. |

Auction proceeds, swap fees, and staking yield all loop back into one treasury — and every Gnar holder votes on where it goes next.

## Contributing

Want to help build? Here's the flow:

1. **Meet your fellow shredders** — hop into the [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/gnars) and introduce yourself. Join discussions, share progress, swap stories. The more, the gnarlier.
2. **Join the Tech POD** — head to the `#contribute` channel and apply. Applications are reviewed manually and unlock the contributor channels.
3. **Explore open issues** — check any repo's Issues tab for something matching your skills, assign it to yourself, and shout it out in `#pod-tech`.
4. **Fork, code, PR** — fork the repo, make your changes, open a pull request, and loop in the Tech POD for review.
5. **Have fun, stay gnarly.** 🤘
